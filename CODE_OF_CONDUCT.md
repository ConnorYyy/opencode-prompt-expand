# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent, but be direct about technical problems and their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information or credentials.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock contributions that violate this standard, and may block repeat offenders. Report concerns privately through the repository's security advisory or the maintainer's public contact method.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific code of conduct. It is not a substitute for GitHub's platform policies.
